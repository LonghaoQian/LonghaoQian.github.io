**Period:** 2021/06 – 2023/04 · Chip Software Engineer, Huawei Technologies Co., Ltd. (Huawei Shanghai Research Institute)

<!-- details -->

- As project lead, led the team in developing the drivers for a next-generation low-power short-range ranging and communication chip and the accompanying smart-home solutions. Outstanding work results and team development; received an A rating in the 2022 annual performance evaluation (top 20% of all staff).
- Outstanding performance in software programming and software standards: obtained Huawei's Professional-Level C++ Software Competency Certification in 2022, and in the same year obtained software Committer qualification, responsible for the team's code-quality control and code-architecture improvement.
- In chip software R&D, led the team in developing scheduling and positioning algorithms for a high-precision short-range ranging chip in C and C++. Combined with the new chip architecture, the novel scheduling algorithm saves 30% of the power consumption of conventional UWB systems. As part of Huawei's smart-home solution, the chip was a key development project of the department.
- In chip software-in-the-loop (SIL) simulation, proposed a fast chip-driver verification method. By simulating chip interrupts, the method reduced software system verification time from 40 minutes to 44 seconds, greatly improving code development efficiency and laying the software foundation for later verification of complex logic. This improvement received the 2022 Huawei HiSilicon Quality Star Award.
- In indoor positioning algorithms, participated in developing an AI-based high-precision positioning method for wearable devices. The method uses a neural network to build a signal multipath-scattering model, effectively distinguishing direct signals from interfering reflected signals and improving ranging accuracy by about 10%.
- Implemented a Wi-Fi-based positioning algorithm with decimeter-level positioning accuracy, which became a strong auxiliary means for the smart-home system.
- In team development, regularly organized team training and technical exchanges, greatly improving the team's skills in C, C++, machine learning, and CMake. Contributed multiple high-quality tutorials to the company's knowledge forum, improving the overall technical strength of the project group.
