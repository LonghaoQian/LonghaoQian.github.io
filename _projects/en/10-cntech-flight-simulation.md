**Period:** 2016/02 – 2020/09 · Software Algorithm R&D Engineer (remote), CNTech — Zhongfang Intelligent Technology (Shanghai) Co., Ltd.

<!-- details -->

- Worked on flight dynamics simulation algorithms and software system R&D, participating in the overall design, testing, and qualification of flight simulation software systems. Built flight simulation software systems covering two mainstream aircraft types, the A320 and the C172, and participated in their debugging and result qualification, laying the foundation for the company's A320 and C172 flight simulation products.
- Wrote, in C++, a simulation tool library that can replace Simulink's modular simulation functionality, making the flight simulation software fully domestically developed. The library not only meets all the requirements of flight simulation but can also simulate general dynamic systems such as spacecraft, power systems, and robotic systems; it also serves as an important functional module for building integrated aircraft design and simulation software, laying the foundation for the company's subsequent independently developed simulation software products.
- Built a range of simulation modules with Simulink, including a six-degree-of-freedom flight simulation model, an aircraft stall simulation model, a landing-gear impact model, a hydraulic power model, and an electrical power system control model, providing very important prototypes and theoretical foundations for the company's promotion, project applications, and engineering projects, and earning high recognition from company management.
- As a senior software algorithm R&D engineer, assisted the company in conducting five rounds of technical interviews, contributing to the selection of new talent.
- Responsible for theory lectures and training for new employees, covering advanced Simulink usage and the implementation of state machines in Simulink.
